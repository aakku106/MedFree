## 3-Minute Elevator Pitch

**Problem:**
"In Nepal, government and NGOs organize free health camps for underserved communities - dental care, eye checkups, vaccinations. But the information is completely scattered across municipalities, health posts, and organizations. Poor people who need these services most can't find them. They miss out on free healthcare simply because they don't know it exists."

**Solution:**
"We built MedFree - a centralized platform that collects health camp data directly from municipalities and makes it discoverable through a PWA. Users can search by location, filter by type of service, and register for camps. It's completely free and open - this is social work, not a business."

## Conversation Flow (Suggested)

### Opening (5 min)

- Thank Sean for his time
- Brief team intros
- Ask: "We'd love to hear about your work with CISPM and social impact projects - what drew you to this call?"

### Your Pitch (5-7 min)

- Problem → Solution → Impact
- Show demo
- Be authentic about challenges

### His Questions (10-15 min)

- Answer honestly
- Don't BS if you don't know
- "That's exactly what we're struggling with" is a good answer

### Your Questions (10-15 min)

- Ask your prepared questions
- Take notes!
- Ask follow-ups based on his answers

### Closing (3-5 min)

- Ask: "What should our next 3 steps be?"
- Ask: "Can we follow up with you in X months with progress?"
- Thank him again

## Key Messages to Convey

### 1. This is About People, Not Tech

- "Every service on our platform represents 50-100 people who could get free healthcare"
- "We interviewed people who walked 2 hours to a health camp only to find it was full"

### 2. We're Serious About This

- "The hackathon is over, but we're continuing to develop this"
- "We're committed to making this work, even without prize money"

### 3. We're Humble and Coachable

- "We're students who built this in 48 hours - we know we have a lot to learn"
- "We want to do this right, not just build features"

### 4. We Care About Sustainability

- "We're thinking long-term - this needs to outlive our graduation"
- "We want to build something the community owns, not just our project"

### 5. We're Values-Aligned

- "This is social work - we have no plans to monetize user data or charge municipalities"
- "Our goal is public good, not profit"

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

### ❌ Don't Say

- "We're going to revolutionize healthcare in Nepal"
- "We just need funding to scale"
- "Our tech stack is so innovative"
- "We're definitely going to win [next competition]"
- "Users love everything about it"

### ✅ Do Say

- "We're tackling one specific problem: health camp discoverability"
- "We're figuring out how to sustain this"
- "We chose simple, reliable tech that works offline"
- "We're learning from this experience"
- "Users find value in X, but struggle with Y"
