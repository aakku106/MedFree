   - Community building (Year of Code parallel)

1. **Don't Oversell Tech**

   - You're students, not product managers
   - Ask for advice, don't defend bad decisions

3. **Don't Focus Only on Awards**

   - "We didn't win but..." is fine
   - What matters is what's next

4. **Don't Ignore Sustainability**

   - He'll ask about long-term viability
   - Have some thoughts, even if incomplete

5. **Don't Waste His Time**
   - Be concise
   - Have clear asks
   - Respect the conversation
