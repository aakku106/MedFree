# Social Impact and Inclusion Challenges in Nepal

This document outlines key social impact and inclusion challenges in Nepal, with a focus on children, people with disabilities, and digital/social accessibility. The information is based on research from various human rights and development organizations.

## 1. Problems Affecting Children

Children in Nepal face a range of issues that hinder their development and well-being.

- **Child Labor and Child Marriage:** The COVID-19 pandemic has exacerbated the problems of child labor and child marriage, reversing previous progress in these areas.
- **Inadequate Social Protection:** The current social protection system is insufficient to protect children from poverty. There are calls to expand the universal Child Grant to provide better support for children.
- **Health and Education:** The pandemic also highlighted the inadequacy of state medical services. Many children, especially those with disabilities, are deprived of basic education.

## 2. Problems Affecting People with Disabilities

People with disabilities in Nepal face significant barriers to social inclusion.

- **Lack of Inclusive Education:** A major issue is the lack of access to inclusive education for children with disabilities. This limits their future opportunities and perpetuates a cycle of poverty and marginalization.
- **Social Stigma and Discrimination:** People with disabilities often face social stigma and discrimination, which can lead to exclusion from community life and economic opportunities.
- **Ineffective Policies:** While there are policies in place to protect the rights of people with disabilities, their implementation is often weak.

## 3. Digital/Social Accessibility and Other Cross-Cutting Issues

Several broader issues affect social inclusion across different groups in Nepal.

- **Digital Divide:** There is a significant digital divide in Nepal, which limits access to information, services, and opportunities, particularly for those in rural areas and from marginalized communities.
- **Lack of Accountability for Human Rights Abuses:** There is a long-standing issue of impunity for human rights abuses committed during the 1996-2006 armed conflict. This lack of accountability undermines the rule of law and perpetuates a culture of violence.
- **Vulnerability of Marginalized Groups:** Women, migrant workers, and members of marginalized communities are particularly vulnerable to abuse, including sexual violence and the denial of their social and economic rights.
- **LGBT Rights:** While there have been legal advances in LGBT rights, their implementation is often lacking, and individuals continue to face harassment and discrimination.
- **Inequality in the Social Protection System:** The social protection system in Nepal has been criticized for reinforcing existing inequalities rather than reducing them.
