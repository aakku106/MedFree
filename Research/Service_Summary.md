# Research: Bridging the Gap in Healthcare Awareness

## 1. Problem

There is a significant information gap regarding free government-provided medical services. Citizens are often unaware of available resources like free checkups and medications, leading to underutilization.

## 2. Proposed Solution

Develop a web application to provide a centralized, real-time database of all free government medical services.

### Key Features

- **Service Listings:** Comprehensive details on free checkups, medications, and health camps.
- **Real-time Updates:** Information is kept current.
- **Notifications:** Users are alerted to new services in their area.

## 3. Data Collection

A network of agents, one for each metropolitan area, will be responsible for collecting information directly from health centers and updating the platform. This ensures the data is timely and accurate.

## 4. Objective

To increase public awareness and utilization of free medical services, thereby improving public health outcomes.
