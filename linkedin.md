## Option 1: Story-First Approach (Recommended)

🏥 **What if the biggest barrier to free healthcare wasn't distance or money—but information?**

A month ago, our team participated in NEPATHONE VOL.1, a 48-hour hackathon in Tilottama, Nepal. We didn't win. But we discovered something more important: a real problem affecting thousands of people in underserved communities.

**The Problem:**
In Nepal, government agencies, INGOs, and NGOs regularly organize free health camps—dental care, eye checkups, vaccinations, general health screenings. These services are completely free and critically needed.

But here's the catch: information about these camps is scattered across municipality websites, health post bulletin boards, Facebook groups, and word-of-mouth. No centralized database exists. The people who need these services most—rural communities, low-income families, elderly citizens—often miss out simply because they don't know the camps exist.

**Our Solution: MedFree**
We built a Progressive Web App that centralizes health camp information from municipalities and health posts across Nepal. Users can:

- 🔍 Search by location and filter by service type
- 📅 See upcoming camps with date, time, location, and capacity
- 📱 Register for services and receive QR confirmation
- 🌐 Access information offline (critical for areas with poor connectivity)
- 🔔 Get notifications about new camps in their area

**The Impact:**
✅ Completely free and open-source
✅ Data verified directly from official sources
✅ PWA works on any device—no app store needed
✅ Admin dashboard for health agents to update in real-time
✅ Supports Nepali calendar (Bikram Sambat)

**What We Learned:**
The hackathon taught us to build fast. But what came after taught us to build for impact. We're now working with municipalities to expand coverage and improve data collection processes.

This is social work, not a business. The database is public—anyone can build on top of it. Our goal isn't to win competitions; it's to ensure no one in Nepal misses out on free healthcare due to lack of information.

- 🎨 **Designers:** Help improve UX for rural users
- 🤝 **Community Members:** Spread the word

## Option 2: Impact-First Approach

---

🌟 **From a 48-hour hackathon to a mission that matters**

Last month, we participated in NEPATHONE VOL.1 under the theme "Social Impact & Inclusion." We chose healthcare accessibility—specifically, the challenge of free health camp discoverability in Nepal.

**The Reality:**
Thousands of free health camps happen across Nepal annually, funded by government, INGOs, and NGOs. Yet the information remains fragmented—posted on random municipality websites, Facebook pages, or physical notice boards. The result? The poorest communities miss out on free healthcare simply because they can't find information.

**What We Built:**
**MedFree** (medfree.adarashagaihre.com.np) - A centralized platform that:

📊 **Aggregates data** directly from municipalities and health posts
🗺️ **Location-based search** - Find camps near you automatically
🎯 **Smart filters** - Search by category (dental, eye, general, etc.)
📝 **Service registration** - Book your spot and get QR confirmation
💾 **Works offline** - PWA technology for rural areas with poor connectivity
🔔 **Push notifications** - Never miss a health camp in your area

**Tech Stack Highlights:**

- Next.js 16 with App Router
- MongoDB for centralized database
- Clerk authentication with offline support
- Progressive Web App (PWA) with service workers
- Geospatial queries for location-based results
- Admin dashboard for health agents

**Open & Free Forever:**
✅ No revenue model—this is social work
✅ Open-source codebase on GitHub
✅ Public database accessible to all
✅ Anyone can build on top of it

**Current Status:**
🚀 Live in production
🎯 Working with municipalities for data expansion
📈 Continuously improving based on user feedback
🌍 Vision: Coverage across all 753 local governments in Nepal
