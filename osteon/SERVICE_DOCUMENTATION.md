# Service Documentation: Free Government Medical Services

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines a new service to be implemented within the Osteon platform. The service aims to address the lack of public awareness regarding free medical services provided by the government. While many valuable services such as free checkups, medical treatments, and medications are available, their reach is limited because citizens are often unaware of their existence, availability, and schedule.

## 2. Problem Statement

The primary problem is the information gap between the government's provision of free medical services and the citizens who are intended to benefit from them. This results in underutilization of these resources, leading to preventable health issues and increased healthcare costs for individuals who could have received free care.

## 3. Proposed Solution

We propose the development of a web-based application that will serve as a centralized, up-to-date repository of all free government medical services. This platform will provide users with easy access to information about available services, helping to bridge the awareness gap.

### 3.1. Key Features

- **Comprehensive Service Listings:** The platform will display a detailed list of all available free medical services, including:

  - Free health checkups
  - Free medications
  - Special health camps and events
  - Vaccination drives
  - Other government-funded health initiatives

- **Real-time Updates:** The information on the platform will be updated in near real-time to ensure accuracy.

- **Search and Filter:** Users will be able to search for specific services or filter them based on location, type of service, and date.

- **Notification System:** A push notification system will be implemented to alert users about new services, upcoming health camps, or important health-related announcements in their area.

## 4. Data Collection and Management

To ensure the information is accurate and timely, a structured data collection process will be established.

### 4.1. Data Collection Workflow

1. **Specialized Agents:** A dedicated agent will be assigned to each metropolitan area. These agents will be the primary point of contact for information dissemination.

2. **Information from Health Centers:** Government health posts, hospitals, and other service centers will be instructed to notify their designated agent as soon as a new free service or event is planned.

3. **System Updates:** The agent will be responsible for immediately updating the web application with the new information. This ensures that the data is live and users have access to the latest details.

## 5. Target Audience

The primary target audience for this service is the general public, with a focus on individuals and families who may have limited financial resources and would benefit most from free healthcare services.

## 6. Success Metrics

The success of this service will be measured by:

- Number of active users on the platform.
- User engagement (e.g., frequency of visits, searches performed).
- An increase in the number of people availing free government medical services (data to be potentially gathered through surveys or from government health centers).
- User feedback and satisfaction.
